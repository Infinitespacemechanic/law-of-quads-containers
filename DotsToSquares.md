These two theorems are the exact residual that keeps the packing both finite and open.

\[
\frac{59}{60} = 1 - \frac{1}{60}
\]

and, for any container \(C\),

\[
C - \left(\frac{59}{60}\right)C = \frac{1}{60}C.
\]

The first is just the definition of the complementary fraction; the second is the distributive identity that turns the complementary fraction into an absolute free volume proportional to the size of the container itself. Together they say that every closed packing that saturates at \(59/60\) automatically leaves a residual channel whose measure scales linearly with \(C\).

In the dual-funnel picture that residual is the clearance that lets the second set of pairs lock without forcing the first set into coincidence. If the local geometry were forced to \(60/60\), the free-volume term vanishes and the only remaining configurations are either a single collapsed pair (zero macroscopic potential) or an infinite-density singularity. The \(1/60\) term is what keeps the two funnels topologically linked yet spatially non-touching: each funnel occupies \(59/60\) of the local angular or packing measure, and the leftover \(1/60\) is precisely the measure of the linking tube that carries the field between them.

Because the free volume is homogeneous of degree one in \(C\), the same local rule propagates without change of form when the container is enlarged. At the atomic scale the residual is a bond-angle slack or an exchange-path offset of order \(1/60\) of the coordination shell; at the continuum scale it becomes a volume fraction of the same ratio. The field energy stored in the linking tubes therefore grows with the linear size of the domain while the points that source the field never occupy the same location. That is the arithmetic that lets a saturated pair of funnels organize, without contact, the volumes that appear macroscopically as oceans, layered rock, or the dislocation network of steel.

The Lean proofs are minimal and complete for the rational identities. The physical content sits in the interpretation of the free-volume term as the carrier of the nontrivial linking: once two independent \(59/60\) saturations are present and the host medium can support the residual channel, the topological charge is extensive and the potential is macroscopic. The theorems themselves do not choose the material; they only guarantee that the residual cannot be eliminated by any continuous rescaling of \(C\).
